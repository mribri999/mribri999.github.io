Privacy Policy

Spintuition does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Effective October 11, 2026

No data collection

  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, or registration.
  • The app does not collect or transmit any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • The app does not connect to the internet or to any server.
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Information stored on your device

Your settings and any sequences you save are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Files you share

You can export sequences (Pulseq .seq files) and videos using the standard iOS share sheet. Sharing happens only when you choose to, and only to the destination you pick. Once you share a file, it is outside the app and anyone who receives it may use it. Likewise, any file you import stays on your device.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to bah@stanford.edu.
